Need auto insurance?

Look for insurance that meets your needs and your budget. There are many things to consider when purchasing auto insurance. It's not always wise to buy the cheapest insurance policy you can find. To find the right policy for your budget, shop around to compare service, products, coverages, as well as price.

Mandatory coverage

Mandatory coverage is comprised of the following coverages: Third Party Liability (Bodily Injury and Property Damage), Direct Compensation - Property Damage, Accident Benefits, Uninsured Automobiles. For third party liability, auto insurance coverage amounts are based on policy limits.
---------

Full coverage

Full coverage is comprised of a mandatory coverage and any of the optional coverages, such as either Collision and Comprehensive, or All Perils coverages. Note that all loss or damage coverages are subject to certain limits, deductibles and exclusions outlined in an auto policy. Contact your broker, or company, for more information about these options.
---------

Are you getting good value for your money?

Consider shopping around if your current insurance costs are in the mid or high range. Ask about coverage as well as costs and compare.

Did you know?

There is a lot to know about auto insurance including how rates are calculated, what products and coverage options are available, and what to do after an accident. As a driver, you have rights and responsibilities which you should be aware of.

Step 4: Shop around

Take the time and contact several insurance providers (company, broker, or agent) to compare coverage and quotes. You will be asked to provide more detailed information about your vehicle and your driving history (at-fault accidents, driving convictions, etc.). This may affect what you will pay for insurance.
